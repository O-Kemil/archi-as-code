## Context

The situation that calls for a decision, and why now. Facts only, each external fact with its source. Say what was verified and what is assumed. Define each technical or architecture notion in one simple sentence the first time it appears.

## Decision drivers

- The needs, constraints and success criteria the options are judged against, numbered so the options can refer to them.

## Options considered

### Option A: name

What it is in two or three sentences. What it brings, what it costs, which drivers it fails. From which point it would become the right choice, if it is not chosen now.

### Option B: name

Same structure.

## Decision

The option chosen and why. Every secondary choice is justified the same way: what it brings, what it costs, and the alternative that was rejected.

## Consequences

- Positive: what becomes possible or easier.
- Negative: what becomes harder or is given up.
- Follow-up: decisions deferred to a later ADR, work that this decision creates.

## Sources

- Links to the official documentation or references used, with the date they were consulted.
